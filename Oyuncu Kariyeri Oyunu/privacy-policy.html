Privacy Policy (English)

Summary: Rise of Greatness respects your privacy. All career progress is stored locally via LocalStorage (with optional Google Play Cloud Save), and we bundle our fonts and assets locally without third-party behavioral analytics or external font CDNs.

1. Data Collection & Usage

Rise of Greatness by RiseUp Studios:

  • Does NOT collect personal identification information (names, emails, phone numbers).
  • Does NOT access your precise location or GPS data.
  • Does NOT access your contacts, camera, photos, or microphone.
  • Does NOT load external web fonts or transmit behavioral analytics to developer servers.

2. Local Storage & Cloud Save

All game data (player stats, career history, matches, and finances) is stored locally via HTML5 LocalStorage. If enabled via Google Play Games Services (OAuth), an encrypted cloud backup is saved to your Google Drive AppData folder. You can permanently delete your local or cloud career at any time via in-game Settings (Deletion Guide).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free gameplay, the app may display optional rewarded and interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may process your device's Advertising ID, coarse IP-based location, and basic ad-interaction diagnostics over encrypted TLS connections with Google LLC (Google Privacy Policy). Where required by law (e.g., EEA/UK), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog is displayed before requesting ads. Pro Pass VIP members enjoy an ad-free experience.

4. In-App Purchases

The Pro Pass VIP upgrade is a one-time lifetime purchase (not an auto-renewing subscription), processed exclusively through Google Play In-App Billing. RiseUp Studios never accesses your financial details.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies.
